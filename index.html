My Favorite Hindi Movies

  1. 3 Idiots

    Ek inspiring aur funny film jo education, friendship aur zindagi ke asli maksad ko dikhati hai. Isme comedy ke saath deep life lessons bhi milte hain.

  2. Lagaan

    Ek powerful period drama jisme cricket, patriotism aur village ki kadi mehnat ko dikhaya gaya hai. Film ek inspiring underdog story hai.

  3. Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge

    Ek classic romantic film jo love, family values aur commitment ka sweet mix dikhati hai. Yeh Bollywood ka ek iconic romance hai.
